Privacy Policy

How Parent Safety collects, uses, protects, transmits, and deletes information.

Privacy at a glance

Parent Safety uses information to provide family binding, safety check-ins, help requests, device-status sharing, and notifications. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Parent Safety Android application (also referred to as “SafeCheck” in some app and technical materials), its supporting backend services, and these public policy pages. “Parent Safety,” “we,” “our,” and “us” refer to the application’s developer and operator.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use Parent Safety, we may process:

  • Profile and family binding information: selected role, names, family relationships, phone numbers you enter, binding codes, and family-link records.
  • Identifiers: generated installation, device, elder, child, user, and family-link identifiers used to connect devices and family members.
  • Safety activity: safety check-in status and time, help-request type and message, recent status, and last-online time.
  • Device status: battery level and the timestamps needed to show whether a linked device was recently online.
  • Notification information: notification permission state, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens, and Huawei Push Kit tokens where applicable.
  • Preferences: offline-alert timing, quiet hours, paused alerts, and emergency-contact settings.
  • Technical information: network request timestamps, error details, and basic server logs needed to operate and protect the service.

Parent Safety does not request access to your address book, precise location, microphone, camera, call history, text messages, financial information, or advertising identifier.

3. Why we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and maintain the app identity associated with your installation.
  • Bind an elder and family member using a temporary binding code.
  • Send and display safety confirmations, help requests, device status, and offline alerts.
  • Open your phone’s dialer when you choose to call a saved family contact.
  • Deliver push notifications and maintain notification preferences.
  • Prevent misuse, troubleshoot failures, maintain service reliability, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Local storage, transmission, and backend processing

Parent Safety stores app state on your device using Android private app storage. Information needed for app identity, family binding, safety check-ins, help requests, device status, alerts, and notifications is transmitted over the network to the Parent Safety backend so the service can store current records and linked family members can receive updates.

Uninstalling the app or clearing Android app storage removes local information but may not remove records stored by the Parent Safety backend. Use the in-app deletion control or contact support to request deletion of associated server records.

5. Service providers and sharing

We use service providers only as needed to operate Parent Safety. Firebase Cloud Messaging, provided by Google, may process FCM notification tokens and delivery information. On supported Huawei builds, Huawei Push Kit may process Huawei notification tokens and delivery information. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process network traffic and operational logs on our behalf.

Safety information is shared with the family member you intentionally bind. We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect people and the service, or as part of a business transfer with appropriate notice. We do not sell personal information, share it with data brokers, or use it for targeted advertising.

6. Retention

We retain information only while it is needed to provide Parent Safety, maintain security, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Retention may vary by data type. When you use “Delete All My Data,” Parent Safety is designed to remove the requesting user’s server records and local information, subject to limited retention required by law, security, fraud prevention, or backup recovery cycles.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • Unbind Family Member removes the family connection while keeping your app identity and settings.
  • Delete All My Data requests deletion of your server records, bindings, check-ins, help requests, notification tokens, and local app data.
  • You may deny notification permission in Android settings.
  • You may contact us through the Support page if you cannot access the app.

To protect users, we may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify that a deletion or access request relates to you. Do not post phone numbers, binding codes, or other sensitive information in a public support forum.

8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. Production data is intended to be transmitted over encrypted connections, access is intended to be limited to authorized systems, and destructive actions are intended to be verified by the server. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children

The “child” role means an adult child or other family caregiver. Parent Safety is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum digital-consent age in the user’s country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use measures intended to protect information during international transfers.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Parent Safety changes. We will revise the effective date and provide additional notice when required. For questions, privacy requests, or deletion help, visit the Parent Safety Support page or email the developer at cliu@tradewind.aero.
