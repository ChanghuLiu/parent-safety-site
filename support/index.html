Help for elders and family caregivers

Support

Setup guidance, troubleshooting, data controls, and ways to get more help.

If someone may be in immediate danger

Contact local emergency services directly. Parent Safety is not an emergency dispatch, medical monitoring, or guaranteed notification service.

01

Set up a family connection

  1. The elder selects the Elder role and opens “My Bind Code.”
  2. The family caregiver selects the Child role and chooses “Add Family.”
  3. Enter the code and confirm the family member’s details.
02

Check in safely

The elder taps “I’m fine” once per day. The linked caregiver can refresh the family status to see the latest confirmation, battery level, and last-online time.

Notifications are not arriving

  1. Open Android Settings and confirm Parent Safety notifications are allowed.
  2. Check that both phones have a working internet connection.
  3. Open Parent Safety on the caregiver phone to refresh its notification token.
  4. Disable battery optimization for Parent Safety if your phone delays notifications.
  5. Open the family status screen and refresh manually.

Binding or status is not working

  • Generate a new binding code if the previous code expired or was already used.
  • Confirm that the elder and caregiver selected different roles.
  • Verify the Parent Safety backend is available and both phones are online.
  • Unbind and bind again only if the existing family connection is incorrect.

Manage your connection and data

Unbind Family Member

Removes the family connection from the server and device. Your app identity and settings remain so you can bind again.

Delete All My Data

Permanently requests deletion of server records, bindings, check-ins, help requests, notification tokens, and local app data. This cannot be undone.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access Parent Safety, email cliu@tradewind.aero with the subject “Parent Safety data deletion request” and explain which device or family connection the request concerns. Do not send binding codes, passwords, government identifiers, or unnecessary medical information.

Contact support

Email the Parent Safety developer at cliu@tradewind.aero. Include your phone model, Android version, Parent Safety version, selected role, what you expected, and what happened. Never include a live binding code or another person’s private information.

Before contacting support, review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
